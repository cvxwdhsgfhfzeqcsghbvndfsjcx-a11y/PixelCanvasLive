2. Stream your camera

Camera stopped

On phones, use the switch button to change between the front and rear cameras. Camera access requires HTTPS and your permission.

3. Share your screen

Screen sharing is optional. Join the queue first, then press Start Screen Sharing if your browser supports it.

Screen sharing is not available in every mobile browser. If it is unsupported, use camera streaming instead.
